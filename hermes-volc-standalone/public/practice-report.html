What became clearer as we talked

China–Laos Railway

What did learning those skills involve?

AT FIRST

China and Laos worked together in its construction and operation. Railway technology and professional skills were shared, and the railway has made travel and transportation easier.

THEN YOU EXPLAINED

During its construction and operation, China and Laos shared railway technology, professional skills and working experience. Many Lao workers received training and learned how to operate and maintain the railway.

Your opening told me that the two countries worked together and that travel became easier. Hearing what Lao workers learned helped me picture what sharing railway skills involved.

The President’s Cat

What made this story meaningful to you?

AT FIRST

The President's Cat is a Georgian novel that was translated into Chinese and introduced to Chinese readers. Through the translation, literature, language and cultural information were shared. The Chinese edition gave more Chinese readers a chance to learn about Georgia.

THEN YOU EXPLAINED

To translate the novel well, Chinese translators needed to learn more about Georgian history, daily life and culture.

YOU CONCLUDED

For me, this story reminds us to stay curious and open-minded when we meet another culture, instead of judging it only from our own point of view.

I could already see how readers benefited. Learning what the translators needed to understand helped me follow your ending about curiosity: it grows out of their need to understand another culture.

Helping your reader follow

What helped me as your listener can help someone reading your story, too.

Who learned what?

The railway story became clearer to me when you explained what the workers learned. When you write about a skill, that kind of detail can help your reader.

Why does that detail matter?

In the novel story, the translators’ need to understand Georgian culture helped me follow your point about curiosity. A connection like that can help your reader understand your view.
